Please go there to get all the latest course information.
You need to 'turn on' your Computer Science Department (CSD) account before you can get into the website when you are off-campus. Please follow the steps below:
- Visit: https://password.cse.ust.hk:8443/pass.html [https://password.cse.ust.hk:8443/pass.html]
- When you reach the "CSD Password Setting Service" page, type a new password and confirm it by typing it again. Please record/remember your password for accessing the course web off-campus in the future.
- Check the options for "Unix account at UG domain" and "PC account at domain CSD", then click "Go UPDATE".
- After 5-10 minutes, your CSD account will be activated.
